Mr Keyboard - en-US dictionary pack: dictionary (main.dict), two-word prediction
(trigrams.bin) and language rules (rules.txt). The first two come from the same
sources below; rules.txt uses the shortcuts of the AOSP list and lists written
for Mr Keyboard.

1) Base word list
dictionaries/en_US_wordlist.combined.gz (version 54) from the Android Open Source Project
(packages/inputmethods/LatinIME), obtained from the mirror
https://github.com/LineageOS/android_packages_inputmethods_LatinIME
(commit c9e8aa87038e1194d2717ac48f207df4068cebd9).

2) New words, frequencies of the new words and word pairs (bigrams)
Counted in the "eng_news_2025_1M" corpus of the Leipzig Corpora Collection (Wortschatz,
Universitaet Leipzig).
D. Goldhahn, T. Eckart & U. Quasthoff: Building Large Monolingual Dictionaries at the
Leipzig Corpora Collection: From 100 to 200 Languages. In: Proceedings of the 8th
International Language Resources and Evaluation (LREC'12), 2012.
https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download
Corpus license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Only counts were taken from the corpus (no sentence is distributed).

3) Chat word pairs
English sentences from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), weekly export
eng_sentences_detailed, with the repeated names and places of the example sentences
(Tom, Mary, Boston...) replaced by separators. License: Creative Commons
Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
Only counts were taken from Tatoeba (no sentence is distributed).

4) Chat abbreviations, contractions and the list of corrections (rules.txt)
The shortcuts (shortcut / not_a_word) of the AOSP en_US list (Apache 2.0, above) and
lists written for Mr Keyboard.

The Hunspell en_US spellchecker was used only as a filter for the new words (American
spelling); nothing from it is copied. Words marked by the AOSP as possibly offensive
do not show up as predictions; the vulgar ones only show up when typed in full.
Tools: docs/tools/split_en.py, make_wordlist_en.py, make_trigrams.py and build_dict.py.
