# Matrix effect of the settings screens

Letters that fall behind the screens of the app, in the colour of the theme (the accent of the app, `mr_accent`; the head of every drop is that colour made lighter). It is a setting of the **Advanced** screen, in the category **Screens**, **off by default**: *Matrix effect* (`pref_ui_matrix`, in the three languages). "Restore defaults" turns it off.

## Where it shows

- **Which screens:** every screen of the app: the settings and the pages of the keyboard settings, the AI settings, the dictionaries, the home screen (`Main`), the word screens (Learned, Import), the tour, the AI style editor, the keyboard sheets and the sheet editor. Only the helper windows (translucent, they only hand something over) do not get it (`MrMatrix.eligible`, `MrScreens.HELPERS`).
- **Where on the screen:** it is the **window background** (`MrRainDrawable`), so it is drawn behind everything. The cards (the rows of the settings, the hero card, the cards of the home screen, of the word screens, of the AI style editor and of the sheets) are **glass** while the effect is on: a soft blue, about 70% opaque so the letters show behind them, lighter at the top (a sheen) with a thin light edge; the letters also show around the cards, behind the category titles and the bars. The bar at the top of the settings screens and the header of the sheets stay solid; the controls (buttons, pills, fields) keep their colour.
- **Home screen (Main) and the other screens:** the same: the rain behind and see-through cards. A root that painted the colour of the screen has no background of its own while the effect is on.
- **Dialogs:** the dialogs of the screens (lists, value bars, text fields, the notes of the version, the update) are a little see-through too (88%); the screen behind them shows, dimmed.
- **Colour:** the flat background of the screens (`mr_ui_bg`) and the letters over it in `mr_accent`; the trail fades away from the head.

## How it works

- `MrRain` is the model, with no Android in it: one drop per column (a head that goes down a row every 1–3 beats, with a trail of 6–22 rows behind it that fades); a drop that has left the bottom comes back after a random wait with another length and speed; a few glyphs change in every beat (the flicker).
- `MrRainDrawable` draws it. It asks for its own beats (80 ms apart, about 12 a second: the drops move in whole rows, so more would only cost battery) with `scheduleSelf`; when the window is hidden (`setVisible(false)`) the beats stop, and they start again on the first draw after it is shown. The glyphs are digits, capitals and signs, plus the half-width katakana when the font has them (`Paint.hasGlyph`, asked once), so there is never a "missing character" box.
- `MrMatrix` decides who gets it and puts it on; `MrScreens` calls it when a screen is created. The effect asks for **hardware acceleration of the window** (the letters are animated; drawn by the processor the whole window would be painted again at every beat). Android only takes that flag when the window is created, so a screen that is already open is **created again** when it comes back after the setting changed (`onActivityResumed`), and the Advanced screen at once (`onSetting`). The manifest is unchanged (`hardwareAccelerated="false"` for the app).
- A failure of any kind (no window, colours that cannot be read, preferences that cannot be opened) leaves the screen as it was: a decoration must never stop a screen from opening. A screen whose rain could not be put is not created again and again: what it was made with is kept and compared.

## See-through cards

The cards do not know about the effect. **Layouts:** the rows (`mr_ui_pref_row*.xml`, `mr_ui_pref_hero.xml`), `main.xml` and the sheets (`mr_sheets_screen.xml`, `mr_sheet_ed_screen.xml`, `mr_sheet_card.xml`, `mr_sheet_ed_test_card.xml`, `mr_sheet_fixed_card.xml`) take their card from the theme (`?attr/mrCard`, `mrCardFlat`, `mrHero`, `mrSheetFixed`, and `mrScreenBg` for a root), declared in `res/values/mr_attrs.xml`. **Screens built in code** (`MrKit`, `MrAiStyleActivity`, the test card of the sheet editor) ask `MrMatrix.wanted(screen)` and take the colour `mr_ui_card_glass` (and a clear background) instead of `mr_ui_card` (and `mr_ui_bg`).

- `Theme.MrKeyboard.Settings` (and the home theme, its child) gives the solid ones (`mr_ui_card_ripple`, `mr_ui_card_shape`, `mr_ui_hero_bg`, `mr_ui_bg`): with the effect off nothing changes.
- The style `MrMatrixGlass` gives the glass ones (`*_glass` drawables: the same files as the solid ones, with the fill replaced by a gradient from the top colour to the bottom one (`mr_ui_glass_top` → `mr_ui_glass_bottom`, 270°) and the line replaced by the light edge `mr_ui_glass_edge`; the corners, the ripple and its mask are the same; the fixed cards of the sheets use the deeper `mr_ui_glass_sunken_*`; the hero card keeps its cyan line and its glow over the glass; `mrScreenBg` clear) and the theme of the dialogs (`android:alertDialogTheme` → `Theme.MrKeyboard.Dialog.Glass`, whose window background is `mr_ui_dialog_bg_glass`, `#e00d1626`, 88%, dark and more solid than the cards). The dialogs that the framework opens (lists, fields) take it from the screen; the two that are opened with an explicit theme (`MrVersion`, `MrUpdateDialog`) ask `MrMatrix.dialogTheme`.
- **In code:** `MrKit` reads `mr_ui_glass_top`, `mr_ui_glass_bottom` and `mr_ui_glass_edge`; its `round(card, line, …)` returns the gradient with the edge for every card (a fill that is the card colour, with a line, not a circle); other fills (buttons, pills with the accent) are as always. `MrAiStyleActivity` uses the flat middle colour `mr_ui_card_glass` for its fields.
- It is a glass **look** (tint, sheen, edge), not a blur of what is behind: Android cannot blur the letters behind a view without a costly capture of the window every frame.
- To make the cards more or less see-through change the colours `mr_ui_glass_*` (and `mr_ui_card_glass`, the middle of the two) and the alpha of the dialog `mr_ui_dialog_glass` in `res/values/mr_colors.xml` (`MatrixTest` keeps the alpha of the cards between 60% and 85%, the top lighter than the bottom, the blue not too light for the text, and the dialog above 80%). The danger rows were already see-through and stay as they are.

## Tests

- `docs/tools/screens_test/MatrixTest.java` (in `run_java_tests.sh`): the drops, trails, return and flicker of `MrRain`; what `MrRainDrawable` paints (colours, faintness, columns), the beats and their stop when hidden; which screens get it; the screen at creation (rain, acceleration and the see-through style, any screen of the app), the dialog theme, the glass colours and drawables, the cards of the screens built in code, after a change of the setting and in the failure cases; the files (attributes, solid and see-through values, drawables, rows, `main.xml`, alpha of the card).
- `res_check.py`: the setting (off by default, after the acceleration, before the read-only rows), the texts in the three languages, the reset list; every `?attr/mr...` used by a layout is declared and has a solid value in the settings theme and a see-through one in `MrMatrixGlass`, and only the settings rows and the home screen use them.

## Test on the phone

Advanced → Screens → *Matrix effect* on: the Advanced screen opens again with the letters falling in the blue of the theme, around the cards and a little visible through them: the cards are a soft glass blue, lighter at the top, with a thin light edge (the text stays readable). Go back to the other settings screens and to the home screen (Main): they have it too, and so do New style (AI), Keyboard sheets, the sheet editor, the word screens and the tour. Open a dialog (a list, the notes of the version): it is a little see-through over the dimmed screen. Turn it off: the letters stop and the flat background is back. Leave the app and come back: the letters keep falling. Do it with the keyboard's *Autocompletar* on and off (the effect does not touch the keyboard).
