# Matrix effect of the settings screens

Letters that fall behind the screens of the app, in the colour of the theme (the accent of the app, `mr_accent`; the head of every drop is that colour made lighter). It is a setting of the main settings screen, in the category **Appearance** (the last row), **on by default**: *Matrix effect* (`pref_ui_matrix`, in the three languages). Nothing is stored until the person changes it (`MrMatrix.enabled` reads `getBoolean(KEY, true)`, the row says `defaultValue="true"`); "Restore defaults" removes the key, so it goes back to on.

## Where it shows

- **Which screens:** every screen of the app: the settings and the pages of the keyboard settings, the AI settings, the dictionaries, the home screen (`Main`), the word screens (Learned, Import), the tour, the AI style editor, the keyboard sheets and the sheet editor. Only the helper windows (translucent, they only hand something over) do not get it (`MrMatrix.eligible`, `MrScreens.HELPERS`).
- **Where on the screen:** it is the **window background** (`MrRainDrawable`), so it is drawn behind everything. The cards (the rows of the settings, the hero card, the cards of the home screen, of the word screens, of the AI style editor and of the sheets) are **a little see-through** while the effect is on, so the letters show behind them; the letters also show around the cards, behind the category titles and the bars. The bar at the top of the settings screens and the header of the sheets stay solid; the controls (buttons, pills, fields) keep their colour.
- **Home screen (Main) and the other screens:** the same: the rain behind and see-through cards. A root that painted the colour of the screen has no background of its own while the effect is on.
- **Dialogs:** the dialogs of the screens (lists, value bars, text fields, the notes of the version, the update) are a little see-through too (88%); the screen behind them shows, dimmed.
- **Colour:** the flat background of the screens (`mr_ui_bg`) and the letters over it in `mr_accent`; the trail fades away from the head.

## How it works

- `MrRain` is the model, with no Android in it: one drop per column (a head that goes down a row every 1–3 beats, with a trail of 6–22 rows behind it that fades); a drop that has left the bottom comes back after a random wait with another length and speed; a few glyphs change in every beat (the flicker).
- `MrRainDrawable` draws it. It asks for its own beats (80 ms apart, about 12 a second: the drops move in whole rows, so more would only cost battery) with `scheduleSelf`; when the window is hidden (`setVisible(false)`) the beats stop, and they start again on the first draw after it is shown. The glyphs are digits, capitals and signs, plus the half-width katakana when the font has them (`Paint.hasGlyph`, asked once), so there is never a "missing character" box.
- `MrMatrix` decides who gets it and puts it on; `MrScreens` calls it when a screen is created. The effect asks for **hardware acceleration of the window** (the letters are animated; drawn by the processor the whole window would be painted again at every beat). Android only takes that flag when the window is created, so a screen that is already open is **created again** when it comes back after the setting changed (`onActivityResumed`), and the screen that has the setting at once (`onSetting`, called by the main settings screen, `LatinIMESettings`, and by the Advanced one for the acceleration). The manifest is unchanged (`hardwareAccelerated="false"` for the app).
- A failure of any kind (no window, colours that cannot be read, preferences that cannot be opened) leaves the screen as it was: a decoration must never stop a screen from opening. A screen whose rain could not be put is not created again and again: what it was made with is kept and compared.

## Nothing runs in the background

The rain only moves while its screen is on display, and costs nothing otherwise:

- **The beats stop with the window.** `MrRainDrawable` asks for a beat only in `draw`, only if it is visible (`isVisible()`), and only one at a time; `setVisible(false)` takes back the beat that was waiting. Android tells the window background when the window is hidden (screen stopped, Home, another app, screen off), and a hidden window is not drawn, so the loop of beat → draw → beat ends by itself.
- **Explicitly too (`MrScreens`).** `onActivityStopped` calls `MrMatrix.pause` (the rain of that screen is set invisible) and `onActivityStarted` calls `MrMatrix.resume`, so that the stop does not depend on what a version of Android or a make of phone does with the visibility of the window. `MrMatrix` keeps the rain of each screen in a weak map (a screen that is gone takes its rain with it); a screen without rain, or one that was never seen, is left alone.
- **Screens under another screen** are stopped, so their rain is stopped; the one on top keeps its own.
- **Animations off on the phone** (the accessibility setting "remove animations", or the battery saver: `ValueAnimator.areAnimatorsEnabled()` is false): no beat is asked for at all, the letters are drawn once and stay where they are. When the animations come back, the next time the window is shown the beats start again.
- **Cost while it runs:** about 12 beats a second (`STEP_MS`), one drop per column with a trail of 6–22 rows, the window drawn by the GPU. The helper windows never have it.

## See-through cards

The cards do not know about the effect. **Layouts:** the rows (`mr_ui_pref_row*.xml`, `mr_ui_pref_hero.xml`), `main.xml` and the sheets (`mr_sheets_screen.xml`, `mr_sheet_ed_screen.xml`, `mr_sheet_card.xml`, `mr_sheet_ed_test_card.xml`, `mr_sheet_fixed_card.xml`, `mr_sheet_empty.xml`) take their card from the theme (`?attr/mrCard`, `mrCardFlat`, `mrHero`, `mrSheetFixed`, and `mrScreenBg` for a root), declared in `res/values/mr_attrs.xml`. **Screens built in code** (`MrKit`, `MrAiStyleActivity`, the test card of the sheet editor) ask `MrMatrix.wanted(screen)` and take the colour `mr_ui_card_glass` (and a clear background) instead of `mr_ui_card` (and `mr_ui_bg`).

- `Theme.MrKeyboard.Settings` (and the home theme, its child) gives the solid ones (`mr_ui_card_ripple`, `mr_ui_card_shape`, `mr_ui_hero_bg`, `mr_ui_bg`): with the effect off nothing changes.
- The style `MrMatrixGlass` gives the see-through ones (`*_glass` drawables, the same files with only the fill changed to `mr_ui_card_glass`, the card colour at 75%, `#c00d1626`; `mr_ui_sunken_glass` for the fixed cards of the sheets; `mrScreenBg` clear) and the theme of the dialogs (`android:alertDialogTheme` → `Theme.MrKeyboard.Dialog.Glass`, whose window background is `mr_ui_dialog_bg_glass`, `#e00d1626`, 88%). The dialogs that the framework opens (lists, fields) take it from the screen; the two that are opened with an explicit theme (`MrVersion`, `MrUpdateDialog`) ask `MrMatrix.dialogTheme`. `MrMatrix.attach` lays it over the theme of the screen (`getTheme().applyStyle`, forced) while the screen is being created, before the layouts are inflated; that is why the effect creates the screen again when the setting changes. A build without the style only loses the see-through cards.
- To make the cards more or less see-through change the alpha of `mr_ui_card_glass` and `mr_ui_sunken_glass` (the same alpha), and of the dialog `mr_ui_dialog_glass`, in `res/values/mr_colors.xml` (`MatrixTest` keeps the cards between 60% and 85% and the dialog above 80%, so the text stays readable). The danger rows were already see-through and stay as they are.

## Tests

- `docs/tools/screens_test/MatrixTest.java` (in `run_java_tests.sh`): the drops, trails, return and flicker of `MrRain`; the setting being on until it is turned off; that a stopped screen asks for no beat (0 beats in 50 tries against 50 when it is on display), that stopping and starting again never leaves two beats waiting, that animations off give one frame and no beats; what `MrRainDrawable` paints (colours, faintness, columns), the beats and their stop when hidden; which screens get it; the screen at creation (rain, acceleration and the see-through style, any screen of the app), the dialog theme, the colours of the screens built in code, after a change of the setting and in the failure cases; the files (attributes, solid and see-through values, drawables, rows, `main.xml`, alpha of the card).
- `res_check.py`: the setting (on by default, in the Appearance category of `prefs.xml`, not on the Advanced screen), the call of `MrScreens.onSetting` in `LatinIMESettings`, the texts in the three languages, the reset list; every `?attr/mr...` used by a layout is declared and has a solid value in the settings theme and a see-through one in `MrMatrixGlass`, and only the settings rows and the home screen use them.

## Test on the phone

A fresh install (or after *Restore defaults*): open the app, the letters are already falling in the blue of the theme, around the cards and a little visible through them (the text stays readable). Settings → Appearance → *Matrix effect* off: the settings screen opens again with the flat background; on: the letters are back. The other screens (home, New style (AI), Keyboard sheets, the sheet editor, the word screens, the tour) have it too. Open a dialog (a list, the notes of the version): it is a little see-through over the dimmed screen. Leave the app (Home, or another app) and come back: the letters keep falling; while it is away, the battery screen of the phone should show no activity of the app. Turn the phone's animations off (Developer options, or *Remove animations*) and open a screen: the letters are there but do not move. Do it with the keyboard's *Autocompletar* on and off (the effect does not touch the keyboard).
